AI Move

📌 Strategic Takeaway — Week of May 11, 2026

ServiceNow's Knowledge 2026 is the most significant SPM-adjacent move this week. Their "Autonomous Workforce" framing — AI that doesn't assist but completes entire processes — combined with bundling all AI by default, raises the stakes significantly. Microsoft's forced Project Online retirement (Sept 30) is a displacement opportunity: thousands of enterprise accounts are in migration mode. Atlassian's CTO shakeup and layoffs create short-term credibility noise despite strong AI product shipping cadence. The consistent theme across all competitors: AI is no longer a premium feature — it's table stakes. Planview's differentiation must lean hard on outcome-based portfolio intelligence and proven enterprise depth.
